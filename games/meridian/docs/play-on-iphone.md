# Play Meridian on your iPhone

Meridian is free and needs no App Store. You add it to your Home Screen from Safari, and then it works like any other app: full screen, with sound, and even without internet.

**Game link:** https://djbatalona06.github.io/GameCentral/

---

## 1. Install it (about 2 minutes)

1. Open the game link above in **Safari**.
   *It must be Safari. Chrome, Google, Instagram or Messages can't install it.*
2. Wait for the title screen to appear.
3. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
5. Close Safari. A **Meridian** icon is now on your Home Screen.

**Always start the game from that icon.** It opens full screen, works offline, and keeps your saves safe.

## 2. First time you play

1. Tap the **Meridian** icon.
2. The **Before you begin** screen lets you pick a setup:
   - **Vision**: bigger text, high contrast, and menus read out loud.
   - **Hearing**: subtitles with names, plus arrows that show where sounds come from.
   - **Motor**: fewer buttons to hold, and more help aiming and climbing.

   Pick one, or none, and tap **Continue**. You can change everything later.
3. Tap **Tap to start**. iPhones only allow sound after your first tap.
4. Tap **New game**, choose an empty save slot, then choose how hard you want it:
   - **Story**: relaxed
   - **Explorer**: normal
   - **Hunter**: hard
   - **Legend**: very hard

## 3. Hold it your way

Play with the phone **sideways (landscape)** or **upright (portrait)**. The buttons move to fit.

If the game won't turn, open **Control Centre** (swipe down from the top-right corner) and switch off **Portrait Orientation Lock** (the padlock with an arrow).

## 4. Controls

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Left thumb** at the bottom left | A stick appears under your thumb. Push to walk; push far to run. |
| **RUN / WALK** (left side) | Tap to switch. In **Walk** mode the stick moves you slowly and quietly (good for sneaking) and never sprints. Tap again to go back to **Run**. |
| **Gun card** (left side, see-through) | Shows the gun you are holding, its name and how much ammo is left. **Tap it to switch guns.** |
| **Goal card** (left side, top) | Shows your current goal. **Tap it** to turn the camera toward where you need to go. |
| **Swipe on the right** | Look around. |
| **JUMP** | Jump, grab ledges, climb. On a ledge, hold the stick in a direction and press Jump to leap that way. |
| **USE** | Use whatever its label says: open, pick up, grapple, push a crate, get in the jeep. |
| **AIM** and **FIRE** | Aim, then shoot. You can drag on Fire or Aim to turn while shooting. |
| **COVER** | Duck behind walls. Tap it while running to roll. |
| **MELEE** | Punch, sneak takedowns, and counters when an enemy flashes white. |
| **THROW** | Hold to see where a bottle or grenade will land; let go to throw. |
| **CROUCH** | Sneak. Hide in tall grass or bushes. |
| **RELOAD** | Reload your gun. |
| **II** | Pause, see your Journal, change Settings. |

Special moments:
- **Driving the jeep (Chapter 4):** push the stick forward to drive and left/right to steer. Push it all the way to boost.
- **Train tunnels (Chapter 6):** when the screen says **"Tunnel! Get down!"**, tap **CROUCH** right away.
- **Searchlights (Chapter 7):** stay out of the beams. If the **Spotted** bar fills up, the guards come. Turn the lights off at their power panels.
- **The bell puzzle (Chapter 5):** every note is written on screen, so you can solve it with the sound off.

## 5. Make it comfortable

Pause (**II**), then **Settings**:

- **Controls → Touch layout:** *Two thumbs*, *One hand (right)* or *One hand (left)*.
- **Controls → Move touch buttons:** drag any button where you want it. Portrait and landscape remember their own positions.
- **Controls → Button size / Button opacity:** make the buttons bigger or easier to see through.
- **Controls → Gyro aiming:** tilt the phone to fine-tune your aim. Your iPhone will ask for permission once; tap **Allow**.
- **Accessibility:** subtitle size, colour-blind modes, high contrast, less camera shake.
- **Audio:** separate volumes for music, effects, voices and world sounds. **Night mode** keeps explosions from being too loud.
- **Video → Quality** and **Target frame rate:** if the phone gets hot or the game feels slow, choose **Low** and **30 fps**.

## 6. Your saves

- The game **saves by itself** at checkpoints. Tap **Continue** on the main menu to carry on.
- Saves live only on this phone. **Deleting the Meridian icon deletes your saves.**
- **To back up a save:** Main menu → **Load game** → **Export**. Pick **Save to Files**.
- **To bring a save back:** Main menu → **Load game** → **Import save file**, then pick the file.

## 7. No internet? No problem

After you've opened the game once with internet, it works in **Airplane Mode** too.

## 8. Getting updates

New chapters arrive by themselves. To check for one:
1. Swipe up from the bottom of the screen and stop in the middle, so all your open apps show.
2. Swipe Meridian away to close it.
3. Open it again while you have internet.

## 9. Something wrong?

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| **No sound** | Flip the **ring/silent switch** on the side of the phone to ring (silent mode mutes web games). Turn the volume up and tap the screen once. |
| **Game is slow or the phone gets hot** | Settings → Video → **Quality: Low** and **Target frame rate: 30 fps**. Close other apps. |
| **Gyro aiming doesn't work** | Turn on **Gyro aiming** in Settings → Controls, and tap **Allow** when asked. |
| **The phone doesn't vibrate** | iPhones don't allow vibration in web games. The App Store version will have it. |
| **I can't find "Add to Home Screen"** | You're not in Safari. Copy the link and open it in **Safari**. |
| **Black screen** | Update your iPhone (Settings → General → Software Update). The game needs iOS 16 or newer. Then close and reopen the game. |
| **Buttons are in an awkward place** | Settings → Controls → **Move touch buttons**, or choose a one-hand layout. |
| **I'm stuck on a puzzle** | Open your **Journal** from the pause menu for clues. After a few tries, the pause menu also offers **Skip this puzzle**. |
