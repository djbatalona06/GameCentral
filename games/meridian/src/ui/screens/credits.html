Meridian is an original game. All characters, places and story are fictional.

Built with TypeScript and Babylon.js (Apache 2.0). Every sound is synthesised in the browser and every texture is drawn in code: there are no third-party art or audio assets.

The menu art and the distant scenery of each chapter were built and rendered in Blender (GPL) by a script made for this game. The desktop app runs on Electron (MIT).

Accessibility features follow the Xbox Accessibility Guidelines and the Game Accessibility Guidelines.
